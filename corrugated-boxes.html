BOX REQUIREMENTS

Specify the job before the format.

A box is selected around what it needs to hold and how it will be stored, handled and shipped. If you have a drawing or sample dimensions, include them with your enquiry. If not, describe the product and the journey it takes.

01

Protection

Describe the packed product, its weight and any parts that need extra care during handling.

02

Dimensions

Share the product dimensions and the space needed for cushioning, inserts or inner packaging.

03

Transit

Explain whether boxes are stacked, stored for a period, moved between sites or shipped to customers.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Product length, width and height

  • Quantity and delivery destination

  • Print, labeling or presentation requirements
